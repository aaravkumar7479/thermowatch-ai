Built for the moment heat becomes risk.

ThermoWatch AI combines NASA FIRMS satellite hotspot data, historical activity, and geospatial context with machine learning to classify and assess thermal events.

Orbital coverage

A rotating constellation revisits every registered site multiple times a day, day or night, through cloud and smoke.

Minutes, not hours

Anomalies are scored and pushed to your team in under six minutes of the pass, before a hotspot becomes an incident.

Risk classification

Every event is ranked and color coded by severity, so your team can triage in seconds instead of reading raw imagery.

Trend intelligence

Historical thermal signatures reveal whether a site is stable, escalating, or repeating a known failure pattern.

Custom geofences

Draw a boundary around any facility, reserve or corridor and get alerts scoped only to what your team owns.

API and integrations

Stream alerts straight into your SCADA, GIS or incident management stack with a documented REST feed.

From satellite detection to actionable intelligence.

Transforming satellite-detected hotspots into clear, actionable intelligence.

01

Detect

NASA FIRMS identifies thermal hotspots from satellite observations and provides their geographic coordinates and detection information.

02

Enrich

Historical hotspot activity and OpenStreetMap geospatial context are combined with the detected hotspot to provide additional information.

03

Classify & Assess

Engineered features are processed by the AI/ML model to classify the thermal event and generate its risk assessment.

04

Visualize

The results are delivered to the GIS dashboard, where hotspots, categories, risk scores and supporting information can be explored on an interactive map.
